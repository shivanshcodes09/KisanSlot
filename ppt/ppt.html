@SIH Idea submission- Template PROPOSED SOLUTION
KisanSlot Platform Architecture

Describe your Idea / Solution / Prototype

An inclusive, multi-channel appointment scheduler transforming chaotic physical queues into a transparent, time-stamped digital token stream.

1. Detailed Explanation

  • Multi-Channel Booking: Farmers reserve slots via smartphone web app, WhatsApp chatbot, or zero-data toll-free IVR call.
  • Capacity Throttling: Mandis configure daily quintal thresholds, dynamically distributing tractor arrivals across hourly windows.
  • Digital Gate Pass: Issues a tamper-proof QR code & 6-digit alphanumeric SMS token containing crop, tare weight & bay info.

2. How It Solves The Crisis

  • Wait Time Slashed 80%: Shifts 48–72 hr highway tractor gridlocks into guaranteed 45-minute gate-in weighbridge slots.
  • Zero Information Asymmetry: Automated broadcast alerts update farmers on moisture acceptance, MSP rates, and schedule adjustments.
  • Transparent Direct Benefit: Live tracking from bag unbagging & quality check to Aadhaar DBT bank credit verification.

3. Innovation & Uniqueness

  • "No-App" Feature Phone Access: Missed call / IVR auto-books slots without requiring internet or smartphones.
  • Dynamic Buffer Algorithm: Automatically recalculates bay throughput when rain delays unbagging or conveyor operations.
  • Anti-Hoarding Token Audit: Integrates farmer land registry numbers to prevent middlemen from hoarding harvest slots.

Practical Ground Implementation

Operational Feasibility & Risk Mitigation

Comprehensive evaluation ensuring practical rollout across rural APMC mandis and procurement centers.

Technical Feasibility

Lightweight web tech works on 2G/3G connections. Offline-first local storage ensures gatekeeper can scan tokens even during temporary internet disconnections.

Economic Viability

Extremely low cloud footprint (free tier Firebase during pilot). Prevents ₹1,200/day tractor idling & diesel waste, delivering high ROI for farming communities.

Social Acceptability

Integrates with existing Gram Panchayat CSCs (Common Service Centres) and Krishi Vigyan Kendras to assist non-smartphone and elderly farmers.
